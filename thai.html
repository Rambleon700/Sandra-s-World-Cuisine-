Slow-Simmered Massaman Beef Curry

Prep time: 20 mins | Cook time: 1 hr 45 mins

Ingredients

  • 600g beef chuck, cut into chunks
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp Massaman curry paste
  • 2 potatoes (cubed), 1/2 cup roasted peanuts
  • 1 tbsp tamarind paste, 1 tbsp palm sugar

Instructions

  1. Fry massaman paste in 3 tbsp coconut cream until fragrant oil splits.
  2. Add beef chunks, toss to coat, then pour in remaining coconut milk.
  3. Simmer covered for 1 hour. Add potatoes, peanuts, tamarind, and palm sugar.
  4. Cook uncovered for 35 minutes until beef and potatoes are fork-tender.
