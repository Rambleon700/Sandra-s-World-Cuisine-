Authentic Spaghetti alla Carbonara

Prep time: 10 mins | Cook time: 15 mins

Ingredients

  • 350g Spaghetti or Rigatoni
  • 150g Guanciale (or cured pancetta), diced
  • 4 large egg yolks + 1 whole egg
  • 80g Pecorino Romano cheese, finely grated
  • Coarsely cracked black pepper

Instructions

  1. Render guanciale in a dry pan over medium heat until golden and crispy; remove pan from heat.
  2. Whisk egg yolks, whole egg, Pecorino, and black pepper together in a bowl.
  3. Cook pasta in boiling salted water until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water.
  4. Add pasta to guanciale pan, remove from heat completely, and vigorously mix in egg mixture with pasta water to form a creamy sauce.
