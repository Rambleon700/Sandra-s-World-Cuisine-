Wok-Fired Chow Mein

High heat, springy noodles, and crisp fresh vegetables.

Classic Supreme Chicken Chow Mein

Prep time: 15 mins | Cook time: 8 mins

Ingredients

  • 300g fresh egg noodles
  • 200g chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup bean sprouts, 2 green onions (chopped)
  • Sauce: 1 tbsp dark soy, 1 tbsp light soy, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1/2 tsp sugar

Instructions

  1. Blanch egg noodles for 1 minute, drain, and toss with a drop of sesame oil.
  2. Heat wok until smoking. Stir-fry sliced chicken for 3 mins, then set aside.
  3. Add noodles to smoking wok, pour in sauce mixture, stir-fry vigorously for 2 mins.
  4. Toss chicken, bean sprouts, and scallions back into the wok for 1 minute before serving.
